# Have & Had Trail

**Designed by Paula Cucurella**

An English game for your phone to practice **have / has / had + past participle**, with 200 academic sentences, long sentences and short paragraphs.

**Play it here:** https://paulacucurella-pixel.github.io/have-had-trail/

## What you practice

- Present perfect: *Numerous studies **have shown** that…*
- Past perfect (pluperfect): *By the time the vaccine arrived, the virus **had spread**…*
- Subjunctive: *I wish I **had chosen** a different topic.* · *Had the authors **known**…*
- Third conditional, modal perfect (*should have*, *must have*), future perfect, perfect passive, and *having* / *to have* + past participle

Each exercise shows its grammar topic in English and Spanish. When you make a mistake, the game explains why and shows the verb's three forms. The exercise comes back a few questions later so you can try again.

## How to play

- **Choose:** tap the correct form.
- **Drag:** drag the words into the gap, or tap them in order.
- **Dogs:** every 5 correct answers brings a new dog to your camp. You start with chihuahuas, and from 35 correct answers each new dog is a bigger breed, up to **Don Corso, the Cane Corso**, at 100.
- Tap a dog to see its name and what it's doing. Tap the grass to throw a ball.
- Your progress is saved in the browser on each device.

The whole game is one file, `index.html`, with no installation needed.
